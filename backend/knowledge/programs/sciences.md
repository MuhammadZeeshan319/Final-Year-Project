## biotech
name: BS Biotechnology
summary: A four-year degree on using living cells, genes and molecules to develop medicines, vaccines and better crops.
studies: cell biology, molecular biology, genetics, biochemistry, microbiology, genetic engineering, bioinformatics
skills: lab techniques, molecular biology methods, research, data analysis
careers: biotechnologist, research scientist, pharmaceutical and agricultural biotech, quality control, further study (MPhil/PhD)
suits: students fascinated by genes and living things who enjoy lab experiments

## micro
name: BS Microbiology
summary: A four-year degree on bacteria, viruses and other microorganisms and their role in health, food and the environment.
studies: general microbiology, bacteriology, virology, immunology, medical microbiology, food microbiology, molecular biology
skills: lab techniques, culturing and identifying microbes, research
careers: microbiologist, hospital and diagnostic labs, food industry, pharmaceutical quality control, research
suits: students who like biology and lab work, especially health and disease

## chem
name: BS Chemistry
summary: A four-year degree on substances, reactions and materials, from basic chemistry to industrial applications.
studies: organic chemistry, inorganic chemistry, physical chemistry, analytical chemistry, biochemistry, industrial chemistry, instrumentation
skills: lab experiments, chemical analysis, using instruments, safety
careers: chemist, quality control in industry, pharmaceutical and textile industry, research, teaching
suits: students who enjoy chemistry, reactions and lab work, and want to work in industry or research

## phys
name: BS Physics
summary: A four-year degree on the fundamental laws of nature, from atoms to the universe.
studies: classical mechanics, electromagnetism, quantum mechanics, thermodynamics, optics, nuclear physics, mathematical physics, electronics
skills: mathematical modelling, problem solving, experiments, analytical thinking
careers: physicist, research, teaching, energy sector, electronics, data science after further training
suits: students curious about how the universe works who love maths and theory

## math
name: BS Mathematics
summary: A four-year degree in pure and applied mathematics - proofs, models and methods used across science, technology and finance.
studies: calculus, linear algebra, real analysis, differential equations, abstract algebra, numerical methods, probability
skills: logical reasoning, proofs, mathematical modelling, problem solving
careers: mathematician, teacher and lecturer, actuarial work, data analysis, research
suits: students who love numbers, proofs and abstract problem solving

## stats
name: BS Statistics
summary: A four-year degree on collecting and analysing data and drawing reliable conclusions from it.
studies: probability, statistical inference, regression, sampling, design of experiments, statistical computing with R, econometrics
skills: data analysis, statistical software, interpreting results
careers: statistician, data analyst, research officer, Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, market research
suits: students who like numbers and finding patterns in data

## envsci
name: BS Environmental Science
summary: A four-year degree on the environment - climate, pollution, water, ecosystems and how to protect them.
studies: ecology, environmental chemistry, climate change, water and air pollution, environmental impact assessment, GIS, natural resource management
skills: fieldwork, sampling and analysis, environmental assessment, report writing
careers: environmental scientist, environmental protection agencies, NGOs, consultancy, climate projects
suits: students who care about nature and climate and enjoy fieldwork outdoors
