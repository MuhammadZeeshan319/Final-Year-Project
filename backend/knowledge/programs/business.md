## bba
name: BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)
summary: A four-year general business degree covering management, marketing, finance, accounting and entrepreneurship.
studies: principles of management, marketing, financial accounting, business finance, economics, organisational behaviour, entrepreneurship, business communication
skills: management, communication, leadership, decision making, teamwork
careers: manager, entrepreneur, marketing executive, HR officer, operations, banking
suits: students who want a broad business education, like leading people or want to start their own business

## bsaf
name: BS Accounting & Finance
summary: A four-year degree in accounting, auditing and financial management, often combined with professional qualifications such as ACCA or CA.
studies: financial accounting, cost accounting, auditing, taxation, corporate finance, financial reporting, business law
skills: accounting, financial analysis, attention to detail, spreadsheets
careers: accountant, auditor, tax consultant, financial analyst, chartered accountant (with CA/ACCA)
suits: students who are comfortable with numbers and detail and prefer a stable, structured career

## bsbf
name: BS Banking & Finance
summary: A four-year degree on banks, investments and financial markets.
studies: money and banking, investment analysis, financial markets, Islamic banking, corporate finance, risk management, economics
skills: financial analysis, understanding markets, numeracy, client handling
careers: banker, investment analyst, financial analyst, credit officer, stock market professional
suits: students interested in money, markets and investment who are comfortable with numbers

## bsmkt
name: BS Marketing
summary: A four-year degree on understanding customers and building brands, from advertising to digital marketing.
studies: consumer behaviour, brand management, digital marketing, advertising, market research, sales management
skills: persuasion, creativity, communication, market research, social media
careers: marketing manager, brand manager, digital marketer, sales manager, advertising
suits: students who enjoy persuading people, creativity and presenting ideas

## bsba
name: BS Business Analytics
summary: A four-year degree combining business with data analysis to support decisions.
studies: statistics, business analytics, databases and SQL, data visualisation, management science, finance, marketing analytics
skills: data analysis, spreadsheets, statistics, business decision making
careers: business analyst, data analyst, operations analyst, consultant
suits: students who like making decisions from data and are comfortable with numbers and statistics

## bshrm
name: BS Human Resource Management
summary: A four-year degree on managing people in organisations - hiring, training, motivating and supporting employees.
studies: human resource management, organisational behaviour, recruitment and selection, training and development, compensation, labour law
skills: people skills, communication, conflict resolution, organisation
careers: HR officer, recruitment specialist, training manager, operations manager
suits: students who enjoy working with people and leading teams
