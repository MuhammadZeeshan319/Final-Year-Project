## mbbs
name: MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery)
summary: The professional degree to become a medical doctor. Five years of basic and clinical sciences followed by a one-year house job in a teaching hospital.
studies: anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, pathology, pharmacology, community medicine, medicine, surgery, paediatrics, gynaecology and obstetrics, clinical rotations
skills: diagnosis, patient communication, clinical decision making, working under pressure, long-term study discipline
careers: doctor, physician, surgeon, specialist after postgraduate training (FCPS), medical officer, public health
suits: students who love biology, want to treat patients directly, are ready for long study and emergencies

## bds
name: BDS (Bachelor of Dental Surgery)
summary: The professional degree to become a dentist. Four years of dental and medical sciences with clinical practice, followed by a house job.
studies: dental anatomy, oral pathology, dental materials, prosthodontics, orthodontics, oral surgery, periodontology, clinical dentistry
skills: precise hand work, patient care, diagnosis of oral disease, attention to detail
careers: dentist, oral surgeon, orthodontist after specialisation, private dental clinic owner
suits: students who like biology and precise manual work, and prefer clinic-based patient care

## pharmd
name: Pharm-D (Doctor of Pharmacy)
summary: A five-year professional degree about medicines - how they are made, how they act in the body and how they are used safely.
studies: pharmaceutical chemistry, pharmacology, pharmaceutics, pharmacognosy, clinical pharmacy, drug regulation, hospital pharmacy
skills: understanding drugs and dosages, lab work, attention to detail, patient counselling on medicines
careers: pharmacist, clinical pharmacist, pharmaceutical industry, drug regulatory authority, medical representative, quality control
suits: students who enjoy chemistry and biology and how medicines work, more than direct emergency care

## dpt
name: Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT)
summary: A five-year degree in physiotherapy - helping people recover movement and manage pain after injury, surgery or illness.
studies: anatomy, physiology, kinesiology, biomechanics, exercise therapy, electrotherapy, neurological and sports rehabilitation
skills: hands-on therapy, exercise planning, patient motivation, understanding of the human body in motion
careers: physiotherapist, sports physiotherapist, rehabilitation centre, private clinic
suits: students who like biology and sport, enjoy helping people recover and working with them one-to-one

## bsn
name: BS Nursing
summary: A four-year degree preparing registered nurses for hands-on patient care in hospitals and the community.
studies: fundamentals of nursing, anatomy, physiology, medical-surgical nursing, paediatric nursing, community health, mental health nursing
skills: patient care, teamwork, calm under pressure, communication, organisation
careers: registered nurse, nurse specialist, nurse educator, community health nurse, work abroad
suits: students who want to care for patients every day in hospitals and want to start working after four years

## mlt
name: BS Medical Lab Technology
summary: A four-year degree in running the laboratory tests that doctors use to diagnose disease.
studies: haematology, clinical chemistry, microbiology, immunology, histopathology, blood banking, lab quality control
skills: lab techniques, accuracy, using diagnostic instruments, analysing samples
careers: medical lab technologist, hospital and diagnostic laboratories, research labs, blood banks
suits: students who like biology and lab work but prefer little direct patient contact

## nutrition
name: BS Human Nutrition & Dietetics
summary: A four-year degree about food, diet and health - using nutrition to prevent and manage disease.
studies: human nutrition, food science, biochemistry, clinical dietetics, community nutrition, food safety
skills: diet planning, counselling, understanding of food and metabolism
careers: nutritionist, clinical dietitian, sports nutrition, food industry, public health programmes
suits: students who like biology and health and want to help people through diet rather than medicine
