## llb
name: LLB (5 years)
summary: A five-year law degree that prepares students to practise as lawyers. Admission usually requires the HEC Law Admission Test (LAT).
studies: constitutional law, criminal law, contract law, civil procedure, Islamic jurisprudence, international law, legal writing, moot court
skills: legal reasoning, argument and debate, reading and writing, public speaking
careers: lawyer, advocate, legal advisor, judge (after experience and exams), corporate law
suits: students who care about justice and rights, love debating and are comfortable with a lot of reading and writing

## psych
name: BS Psychology
summary: A four-year degree on how the mind works and how people think, feel and behave.
studies: general psychology, developmental psychology, abnormal psychology, social psychology, research methods, counselling, psychological testing
skills: understanding people, listening, research, counselling basics
careers: psychologist (clinical after further study), counsellor, HR, school psychologist, research
suits: students curious about the mind who prefer listening and helping individuals

## econ
name: BS Economics
summary: A four-year degree on how economies, markets and governments work, using theory and data.
studies: microeconomics, macroeconomics, mathematical economics, statistics, econometrics, development economics, public finance
skills: data analysis, economic reasoning, policy analysis
careers: economist, State Bank and banking sector, policy research, civil service (CSS), development organisations
suits: students interested in money, markets and policy who are comfortable with maths and data

## ir
name: BS International Relations
summary: A four-year degree on world politics, foreign policy, diplomacy and international organisations.
studies: international politics, foreign policy of Pakistan, diplomacy, international law, international organisations, security studies
skills: analysis of world events, writing, negotiation, presentation
careers: diplomat (Foreign Service via CSS), international organisations, think tanks, journalism, civil service
suits: students fascinated by world politics who enjoy reading, writing and debating

## polsci
name: BS Political Science
summary: A four-year degree on governments, political systems, public policy and political ideas.
studies: political theory, comparative politics, Pakistan politics, public policy, international relations, research methods
skills: analysis, writing, debating, understanding of government
careers: civil servant (CSS), policy analyst, journalism, politics, research
suits: students interested in government and policy who enjoy reading and debate

## pubadmin
name: BS Public Administration
summary: A four-year degree on how government departments and public organisations are managed.
studies: public administration, public policy, public finance, governance, administrative law, human resource management in the public sector
skills: management, policy implementation, organisation, communication
careers: civil servant (CSS/PMS), government officer, NGO management, policy implementation
suits: students who want to work in government and run public services

## socio
name: BS Sociology
summary: A four-year degree on society, communities, social problems and change.
studies: sociological theory, social research methods, social problems, gender studies, rural and urban sociology, criminology
skills: research, understanding communities, writing, data collection
careers: sociologist, development sector and NGOs, research, civil service, social policy
suits: students interested in society and communities who want to understand social problems

## socialwork
name: BS Social Work
summary: A four-year degree preparing students to support individuals, families and communities in need.
studies: social work practice, community development, human behaviour, social policy, counselling, field work
skills: helping people, counselling, community organisation, empathy
careers: social worker, NGOs, community development, hospitals and welfare organisations
suits: students who want to help people directly and work with communities
