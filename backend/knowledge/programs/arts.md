## bsedu
name: BS Education
summary: A four-year degree preparing teachers - how students learn and how to teach effectively.
studies: educational psychology, teaching methods, curriculum development, classroom management, assessment, teaching practice
skills: explaining clearly, lesson planning, classroom management, patience
careers: school teacher, educator, curriculum developer, education administrator
suits: students who love explaining things to others and want to work with children and students

## masscomm
name: BS Mass Communication
summary: A four-year degree on journalism, media and communication - news, TV, radio, advertising and digital media.

skills: writing, reporting, interviewing, video and audio production, communication
careers: journalist, news reporter, TV producer, public relations, digital content creator, advertising
suits: students who like telling stories, following the news and working to fast deadlines
