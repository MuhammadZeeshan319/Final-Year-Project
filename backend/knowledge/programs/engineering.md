## ee
name: BE Electrical Engineering
summary: A four-year engineering degree about electricity, electronics, power systems and communication.
studies: circuit analysis, electronics, digital logic design, signals and systems, power systems, electrical machines, control systems, telecommunication
skills: circuit design, mathematical analysis, programming microcontrollers, problem solving
careers: electrical engineer, power sector (WAPDA, K-Electric), electronics design, telecom engineer, renewable energy
suits: students strong in physics and maths who are excited by circuits, power and electronics

## me
name: BE Mechanical Engineering
summary: A four-year engineering degree about machines, engines, energy and manufacturing.
studies: engineering mechanics, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, machine design, manufacturing processes, heat transfer, CAD
skills: design and analysis of machines, CAD drawing, hands-on building, problem solving
careers: mechanical engineer, automotive industry, manufacturing, HVAC, power plants, maintenance engineer
suits: students who like physics, forces and motion and enjoy building and taking things apart

## ce
name: BE Civil Engineering
summary: A four-year engineering degree about designing and building infrastructure - buildings, bridges, roads, dams and water systems.
studies: structural analysis, concrete and steel design, surveying, geotechnical engineering, hydraulics, transportation engineering, construction management
skills: structural design, site management, surveying, calculation, teamwork on projects
careers: civil engineer, structural engineer, construction projects, government works departments, consultancy
suits: students who like maths and physics and want to work on large projects, often outdoors on site

## che
name: BE Chemical Engineering
summary: A four-year engineering degree about industrial processes that turn raw materials into products such as fuel, fertiliser, food and medicines.
studies: chemistry for engineers, thermodynamics, mass and heat transfer, reaction engineering, process control, plant design
skills: process design, chemistry, data analysis, safety management
careers: chemical engineer, fertiliser and oil and gas industry, pharmaceutical and food plants, process engineer
suits: students who enjoy chemistry and maths and want to work in factories and industrial plants

## mte
name: BE Mechatronics Engineering
summary: A four-year engineering degree combining mechanical, electrical and computer engineering to build robots and automated systems.
studies: mechanics, electronics, microcontrollers, control systems, robotics, sensors and actuators, programming, automation
skills: robot design, programming, electronics, system integration
careers: robotics engineer, automation engineer, industrial control, product development
suits: students who love robots and automation and enjoy both coding and building hardware

## arch
name: B.Arch (Bachelor of Architecture)
summary: A five-year professional degree in designing buildings and spaces that are useful, safe and beautiful.
studies: architectural design studios, drawing and model making, building construction, history of architecture, structures, urban design, CAD and 3D modelling
skills: creative design, drawing, spatial thinking, presenting ideas, 3D software
careers: architect, interior design, urban planning, landscape design, design consultancy
suits: creative students who enjoy drawing and geometry and want to design buildings rather than calculate them
